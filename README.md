## The entry

A single one-line 1-star review, written in the voice of a houseplant
reviewing its human owner (under 280 characters, gate: 1–279):

> One star: he bought me for 'good energy', then parked me behind the Wi-Fi router for feng shui and now blames my brown leaf tips on my attitude.

Why this line: it is a genuine review — a clear complaint (bought for
"good energy", then parked behind the router) and a reason for the single
star (the owner blames the plant's brown leaf tips on its attitude). The
grievance is the plant's own, told through plant details (light, placement,
leaf tips), with a surprising turn instead of the stock wilting/neglect
lines the judge brief warns against.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` and refuses to emit it if
it ever exceeds the 279-character gate.

Run it with Node (or any TypeScript runner):

```sh
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
```
